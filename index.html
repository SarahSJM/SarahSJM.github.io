Our Story

It all started in first year, sharing a flat as completely strict, sensible, totally-not-interested-in-each-other flatmates with 2 of our closest friends. That bulletproof friendship lasted all year until one summer holidays night out in Glasgow, where a few drinks and a lot of late-night chatting revealed there might actually be a spark hiding behind the strict rules of do not date your flatmate.

As we were licing together again in 2nd year, we naturally did the most rational thing possible: initiated a top-secret romance. Living under the same roof meant playing it completely cool to avoid blowing up the flat dynamics if things went sideways. But after months of sneaking around our own hallway, we finally gave in, and admitted to everyone—and ourselves—that this might be my person.

And we are so happy to have you all here for the start of our forever!

Travel & Transport

By Train

The nearest train station is Markinch Station, which has direct links from Edinburgh, Dundee, and Aberdeen. Balbirnie House is approximately a 15 to 20-minute walk (around 0.8 miles) through the park grounds from the station.
