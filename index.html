Our Story 💕

It all started in first year, sharing a flat as completely strict, sensible, totally-not-interested-in-each-other flatmates 🏠. That bulletproof friendship lasted until one fateful night out in Glasgow 🍸, where a few drinks and a lot of late-night chatting revealed there might actually be a spark hiding behind the dirty dishes and shared fridge space!

Naturally, we did the most rational thing possible: initiated a top-secret romance 🕵️‍♂️. Living under the same roof meant playing it completely cool to avoid blowing up the flat dynamics if things went sideways. But after months of sneaking around our own hallway, we finally gave in, dropped the secret agent act, and admitted to everyone—and ourselves—that we were head over heels in love! 🥰

Travel & Transport 🚗

By Train 🚆

The nearest train station is Markinch Station, which has direct links from Edinburgh, Dundee, and Aberdeen. Balbirnie House is approximately a 15 to 20-minute walk (around 0.8 miles) through the park grounds from the station 🚶‍♂️🚶‍♀️.
